What is Palorant?

Enter the world of Palorant, a tactical FPS game that tests your strategy and reflexes. Choose your favorite agent, master their unique abilities, and engage in intense, action-packed battles. Work with your team to outsmart opponents and secure victory!

Meet the Top Agents in Palorant!

Sova

Role: Initiator

Uses recon abilities to reveal enemy positions and scout areas.

  • 🔍 Owl Drone – Deploys a drone to scout areas and tag enemies.
  • 🎯 Shock Bolt – Fires a bolt that explodes and deals area damage.
  • 📡 Recon Bolt – Reveals enemy positions in its radius.
  • 🌩️ Hunter's Fury (Ultimate) – Fires energy blasts through walls.

Brimstone

Role: Controller

Uses smokes and fire-based abilities to control the battlefield and block vision.

  • ☁️ Sky Smoke – Calls in long-range smokescreens on the map.
  • ⚡ Stim Beacon – Gives rapid-fire boost to nearby teammates.
  • 🔥 Incendiary – Launches a molotov grenade that deals fire damage.
  • 💥 Orbital Strike (Ultimate) – Massive laser strike in selected area.

Sage

Role: Sentinel

Heals and revives teammates, and can block paths with barriers.

  • 🧊 Slow Orb – Slows and silences enemies walking over it.
  • 🧱 Barrier Orb – Creates a large wall to block paths.
  • 💚 Healing Orb – Heals herself or teammates over time.
  • 💖 Resurrection (Ultimate) – Revives a dead ally.
