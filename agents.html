Choose Your Agent – Define Your Playstyle!

Each agent in Palorant brings a unique strategy and playstyle to the battlefield. Choose the agent that suits your tactics!

Sova

Role: Initiator

Uses recon abilities to reveal enemy positions and scout areas.

  • 🔍 Owl Drone – Deploys a drone to scout areas and tag enemies.
  • 🎯 Shock Bolt – Fires a bolt that explodes and deals area damage.
  • 📡 Recon Bolt – Reveals enemy positions in its radius.
  • 🌩️ Hunter's Fury (Ultimate) – Fires energy blasts through walls.

Brimstone

Role: Controller

Uses smokes and fire-based abilities to control the battlefield and block vision.

  • ☁️ Sky Smoke – Calls in long-range smokescreens on the map.
  • ⚡ Stim Beacon – Gives rapid-fire boost to nearby teammates.
  • 🔥 Incendiary – Launches a molotov grenade that deals fire damage.
  • 💥 Orbital Strike (Ultimate) – Massive laser strike in selected area.

Sage

Role: Sentinel

Heals and revives teammates, and can block paths with barriers.

  • 🧊 Slow Orb – Slows and silences enemies walking over it.
  • 🧱 Barrier Orb – Creates a large wall to block paths.
  • 💚 Healing Orb – Heals herself or teammates over time.
  • 💖 Resurrection (Ultimate) – Revives a dead ally.

Reyna

Role: Controller

A poison-based agent who uses toxic smokes and abilities to control zones and block enemy vision.

  • 💀 Leer – Casts an ethereal eye that nearsights all who look at it.
  • 💢 Devour – Heals rapidly after a kill using soul orb.
  • 🌫️ Dismiss – Become intangible using a soul orb after kill.
  • 🩸 Empress (Ultimate) – Enter a frenzy state with rapid healing and speed.

Phoenix

Role: Duelist

A self-sufficient agent with healing and damage abilities, capable of reviving himself with his ultimate.

  • 🔥 Curveball – Throws a blinding fireball that curves left or right.
  • 🧱 Blaze – Creates a flame wall that blocks vision and heals Phoenix.
  • 🖐️ Hot Hands – Throws fire to damage enemies and heal himself.
  • ☀️ Run It Back (Ultimate) – Marks a respawn point to revive upon death.

Cypher

Role: Sentinel

A trap-based agent who uses cameras and tripwires to gather intel and control areas.

  • 📹 Spycam – Places a camera to watch and mark enemies.
  • ⚠️ Trapwire – Traps enemies passing through and reveals them.
  • 🕵️ Cyber Cage – Creates a zone that obscures vision and slows.
  • 💀 Neural Theft (Ultimate) – Reveals all enemy positions from a corpse.

Rift

Role: Controller

A dimension-warping specialist who can create teleportation gates and distort enemy vision.

  • 🛠️ Turret – Deploys a turret that fires at enemies in a cone.
  • 🧠 Alarmbot – Sends out a bot that debuffs and explodes on contact.
  • 📦 Nanoswarm – Throws a grenade that releases a damaging swarm.
  • 🚨 Lockdown (Ultimate) – Detains all enemies in range after a delay.

Kania

Role: Duelist

A time-bending warrior who can slow down enemies and rewind their own movements.

  • ⏳ Time Warp – Creates a temporal field that slows enemies.
  • 🔁 Echo Step – Allows Kania to retrace her steps from a few seconds ago.
  • 🌀 Phase Drift – Enters a ghost-like state, avoiding damage briefly.
  • 🕰️ Chrono Break (Ultimate) – Rewinds time for herself and nearby allies to a previous health and position state.
